# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SimpleNotes welcomes respectful, constructive collaboration. The project should be a place where contributors can ask questions, report problems, propose changes, and disagree on implementation details without personal attacks.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and respectful.
- Critique code and decisions, not people.
- Explain technical feedback with evidence and actionable suggestions.
- Respect user privacy and do not publish note contents or personal data.
- Respect intellectual property and software licences.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, personal attacks, and deliberate disclosure of private or security-sensitive information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report serious incidents privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.
